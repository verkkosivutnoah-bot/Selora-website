What we do

Nine things we get called for most

Every job starts the same way — a look at what's actually there, then the smallest change that fixes it properly.

Energy savings

Practical advice and work that cuts kilowatt-hours — starting with the loads that actually dominate your bill.

Knob & tube / aluminum

Rewiring outdated knob-and-tube and aluminum branch circuits over to copper.

EV charger (EVSE)

Class 2 electric car charger installation, sized and fed correctly from the panel.

Emergency backup power

Standby generator installation so the essentials keep running when the grid stops.

LED cupboard lighting

Under-cabinet and kitchen cupboard LED lighting, wired in and switched properly.

Pot & down lights

LED potlight and downlight layouts installed cleanly, with minimal ceiling damage.

LED strip & tape

Fans, dimmers & sensors

Ceiling fans, dimmers, timers and motion detectors — supplied, mounted and set up.

Whole-house surge protection

Why "green"

Doing more with less is the main engine of our operation.

  • Small vehiclesOur vehicles are small and energy efficient — and easy to notice on the street.
  • Smart toolsTools kept as small and as smart as possible, so less gets hauled and less gets used.
  • Waste separatedAll recyclable waste is separately disposed of.
  • No paper adsWe do not force anyone to cut trees — we rely on the internet and word of mouth.

Save kilowatt-hours

Where the savings actually are

In 2015 Canada was the fifth-largest consumer of electricity per capita at 15,542 kWh a year — against a world average near 2,700. These are the changes that move the needle most.

01
Electric stoveSwitch to a gas stove and use gas sparingly.
02
Water heaterSwitch to tankless gas or solar, and use it sparingly.
03
Home heatingSwitch to gas, set the programmable thermostat low, add ceiling fans — and insulate.
04
Clothes dryerSwitch to gas or solar drying, used sparingly.
05
Large appliancesEnergy Star fridge, freezer, dishwasher and microwave.
06
Small appliancesEnergy Star hair dryer, toaster, flat iron — and use them sparingly.
07
TVs & screensA moderate size set using LED only. LCD and plasma are the blast from the past.
08
LightingLED bulbs and fixtures use about an eighth of the energy of incandescent.
09
