01 — What we do

Six rooms, one idea: find it early.

Preventive & wellness

Annual exams, vaccination planning and bloodwork that catches the slow problems while they are still small.

Dentistry

Scaling, extractions and charting under monitored anaesthesia — with pre-op bloods every single time.

Soft-tissue surgery

Routine and referred procedures in a dedicated theatre, with a nurse whose only job is watching your dog breathe.

Imaging & diagnostics

Digital radiography and ultrasound on site, so you get an answer in the same visit rather than the next one.

Senior care

Mobility, kidneys, hearts and comfort. Older dogs get longer appointments because they need them.
