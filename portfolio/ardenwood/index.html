How it goes

Four conversations, then a sale.

  1. 01

    The walk

    Two hours, no valuation talk. I need to see the house the way a buyer will — where the light lands, what you apologise for, what you are proud of.

  2. 02

    The number

    A written position with comparable evidence, the three prices I think it trades at, and which of them I would actually take. No flattery pricing.

  3. 03

    The work

    Styling, survey, photography at the right hour, and a private release to my list before anything goes public. Most of my sales happen in this window.

  4. 04

    The close

    I run every negotiation myself and you hear from me the day something changes, including when nothing has. Through to keys.
